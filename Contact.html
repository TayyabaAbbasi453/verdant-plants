Common Questions

FAQ

How long does delivery take?

We process and dispatch orders within 24 hours. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days across Pakistan. Express 1–2 day options are available at checkout.

What if my plant arrives damaged?

We guarantee every plant arrives healthy. If yours doesn't, just email us a photo within 48 hours of delivery and we'll replace it or issue a full refund — no questions asked.

Do you offer wholesale or bulk pricing?

Yes! We have a dedicated wholesale programme for offices, interior designers, hotels, and event planners. Get in touch via the contact form above, selecting "Wholesale / Bulk Order."

Can I return a plant if I change my mind?

We accept returns within 14 days for unopened, unused items in original packaging. Because plants are living organisms, we assess returns on a case-by-case basis — reach out and we'll help.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently we ship within Pakistan only. International shipping involves complex phytosanitary requirements; we're working on it and will announce when available.
