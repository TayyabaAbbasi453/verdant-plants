Founded 2019

We Started With a Single Leaf

Verdant began in a Karachi apartment with a windowsill of rescued cuttings and a dream: to make premium plant parenthood accessible to everyone. What started as weekend trips to nurseries turned into a curated marketplace connecting plant lovers with the healthiest, most beautiful specimens available.

Today we partner with over 40 small-batch growers across South Asia, hand-selecting every variety we carry. Each plant is inspected, hardened off in our facility, and shipped within 24 hours of your order.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To make nature an everyday luxury — delivering healthy, responsibly grown plants with the same care we'd give our own homes, while building a community where plant parenthood is joyful and approachable for all.

Our Vision

A world where every home has at least one thriving plant — and where that plant came from a grower who was paid fairly, a supply chain that minimised carbon, and a brand that cared about the whole journey from seed to shelf.

Planet First

Our Commitment to Sustainability

100% Recyclable Packaging

Every box, liner, and insert we use is either recycled, recyclable, or compostable.

Peat-Free Growing

We source plants grown in peat-free substrates to protect vital peatland ecosystems.

Water-Smart Practices

Our partner growers use drip irrigation and rainwater capture to reduce water waste by 60%.

Fair Trade Partnerships

Every grower in our network is vetted for fair wages and ethical working conditions.
